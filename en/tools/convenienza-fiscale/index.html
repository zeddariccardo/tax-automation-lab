The Convenienza fiscale is available in Italian only

The tools are being consolidated on a single codebase. While that work is under way they are maintained in Italian only: keeping three translated copies of an application that changes every week would mean shipping fixes to one language and not the others.

Open the tool (in Italian) → Back to the tools

The tool compares the net personal cash of an Italian lawyer or chartered accountant across the flat-rate regime, ordinary self-employment and a professional limited company. The rules it applies — the 85,000 euro threshold, the 78% profitability coefficient, Cassa Forense and CNPADC contributions, the withholding on dividends — are specific to Italian law and have no meaningful equivalent elsewhere. It runs entirely in your browser and the files you produce are yours.

English and Spanish versions will return once the tools stop changing.
